OPEN QUESTIONS, pending answers from the laboratory
(each one blocks a specific use of the data; none are stylistic)

1. Hardness units. "25" is reported without a unit. 25 mg/L CaCO3 is very soft
   water; 25 °dH is very hard water -- a factor of about 18 between the two
   readings. Until this is answered the hardness column cannot be interpreted
   at all, and it is stored with unit `unknown`.

2. Nitrite: is the figure reported as NO2- or as NO2-N? The two differ by a
   factor of 3.28, and the distinction decides whether the values sit above or
   below the WHO guideline.

3. Chlorine units -- presumed mg/L, not stated.

4. Methods and detection limits for every analyte. Several results are 0.00,
   which is almost certainly "below the detection limit" rather than a true
   zero; without the limit they cannot be recorded correctly (see
   data/README.md section 3, non-detects).

5. Sulfate for sample 4 was reported twice: 54 in the routine series and 65.7
   as a "точная проба" (reference measurement). Which method produced which
   figure, and does the same bias apply to the other five samples, which were
   only measured by the routine method?

6. Analyst name for the record, and the analysis date.
